# Native / meme formats (works for any audience)

These are made to look like posts, not ads. Paste the Brand block from `README.md` under every prompt.

## N1. Two-panel No / Yes
**Prompt:** Original two-panel meme image, square panels stacked vertically, ivory background, clean black sans serif text. Top panel: a cartoon cat looking exhausted surrounded by four floating browser windows labeled "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek", with the caption "Me switching between 4 AI tabs". Bottom panel: the same cat relaxed with sunglasses next to one window showing the attached MODUS logo, with the caption "Me on MODUS". Flat illustration style, no real people, no copyrighted characters.
**Why:** meme formats get shared and commented on, which lowers cost per click.

## N2. Bracket
**Prompt:** Clean sports-style tournament bracket graphic on ivory paper texture. First round: "GPT" vs "Claude" and "Gemini" vs "DeepSeek". The final box in the middle holds the attached MODUS logo with the text "Why pick one?". Bold black title at the top reading exactly: "AI bracket." Small text at the bottom reading exactly: "All four in one app. $24."
**Why:** the format invites people to comment their pick, and comments boost reach.

## N3. Scoreboard
**Prompt:** Photo of a retro stadium scoreboard at dusk with warm bulb lights. Left side reads "4 APPS" with a scrolling list "GPT CLAUDE GEMINI DEEPSEEK". Right side reads "1 APP" with the attached MODUS logo. Center clock reads "$24". Cinematic, slightly grainy. No other text.
**Why:** bold and different from every other AI ad in the feed.

## N4. Notes app
**Prompt:** Clean screenshot-style image of a minimal light-mode phone notes app (generic, no brand), title "AI subscriptions", with four checklist lines: "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek", each crossed out. Under them, one new unchecked line: "MODUS (has all 4)". Phone status bar at the top showing 9:41. Nothing else.
**Why:** looks like someone's real note, so it reads as authentic.

## N5. Tweet-style quote card
**Prompt:** Clean light-mode social post card on an ivory background, generic layout with no platform logo. A gray circle avatar, name "a founder". Post text in black: "cancelled 3 AI subscriptions. got one app with GPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek. same answers, one bill." No like counts, no other text.
**Note:** this must stay clearly illustrative. Do not invent fake engagement numbers or a real person's name, because Meta treats fake testimonials as deceptive. Better: replace the text with a real quote from Oliver, Jian or icy if they'll give you one.
**Why:** social proof format, once it's backed by a real quote.

## N6. Spotify-style "wrapped" card
**Prompt:** Bold flat graphic card in violet #7C3AED and ivory, year-in-review style. Title "Your AI year". Four stat rows in big type: "GPT: 212 questions", "Claude: 148 questions", "Gemini: 97 questions", "DeepSeek: 41 questions". At the bottom: "All in one app." with the attached MODUS logo. Playful, confident layout, no other brand marks.
**Why:** a familiar year-in-review format, and the timing works in November and December.
