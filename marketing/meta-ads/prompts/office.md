## O1. Sticky-note logins
**Prompt:** Photorealistic close-up of an office monitor bezel covered with four yellow sticky notes in handwriting: "GPT login", "Claude login", "Gemini login", "DeepSeek login". A hand is peeling them all off at once. Fluorescent-free soft daylight, real desk clutter. Bold black text on a clean ivory band at the top reading exactly: "Four logins. Or one."
**Why:** an instantly recognizable office annoyance.

## O2. Monday morning
**Prompt:** Candid over-the-shoulder photo of a person in a knit sweater at a kitchen counter at 8am holding a coffee mug, a laptop open in front of them slightly out of focus. Warm window light, film grain, authentic, not stock-photo looking. Clean white sans serif text across the upper third reading exactly: "Asked my AI what my week looks like. It checked my calendar." Small text at the bottom reading exactly: "MODUS. Every model, connected to your work."
**Why:** shows the connected-calendar action, which plain chat apps can't match in one line.

## O3. Hard email [REAL UI optional]
**Prompt:** Photorealistic photo of a laptop on an office desk showing an email draft window titled "Re: budget" in a generic light email interface with no real brand. Three sticky notes stuck along the bottom edge of the screen read "GPT's draft", "Claude's draft" and "Gemini's draft", one ticked with a violet pen mark. Calm late-afternoon light. Bold black text at the top reading exactly: "Three drafts. Send the best one."
**Alt:** use your real Compare screenshot with a "write a polite email declining this meeting" prompt.
**Why:** every office worker has written a hard email this week.

## O4. Team meeting
**Prompt:** Photorealistic photo of a bright meeting room. On the wall screen, a simple slide reads "Today's agenda: your question". Around the table, four laptops with plain stickers on their lids reading "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini" and "DeepSeek", no people. Clean modern office, soft daylight. Bold black text on a clean band at the top reading exactly: "Your best team meeting has no people in it."
**Why:** a little funny and very scroll-stopping in a work context.
