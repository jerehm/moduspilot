## F1. Whiteboard
**Prompt:** Photorealistic photo of an office whiteboard. At the top in black marker: "Which AI is best for this?" with a bold red line through it. Under it in violet marker: "Ask 3. Keep the best." with an arrow pointing to three small sketched boxes labeled "GPT", "Claude" and "Gemini". The edge of a laptop and a coffee cup are visible at the bottom. Natural office light. No other text.
**Why:** founders already argue about which model to use. This settles it in one image.

## F2. Creator desk
**Prompt:** Photorealistic photo of a creator's desk at golden hour: a ring light, a phone on a small tripod, a microphone and a printed script page covered in pen edits. Three colored sticky notes are stuck to the script, reading "GPT's hook", "Claude's hook" and "Gemini's hook", one circled in violet. Warm, aspirational and real. Bold white text at the top reading exactly: "Write the hook three ways."
**Why:** creators test hooks all day. This shows the exact use case.

## F3. Pitch deck
**Prompt:** Photo of a printed pitch deck slide pinned to a corkboard, the slide reading "Why now?" in large type. Four sticky notes around it, each with a different short handwritten answer and a tiny label: "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek". One note has a violet checkmark. Soft daylight, startup office feel. Bold black text on a clean band at the top reading exactly: "Second opinions, built in."
**Why:** shows a founder's actual high-stakes moment.
