## B1. Four into one
**Prompt:** Clean studio product photo on a warm ivory surface. Four matte rounded-square tiles in a row, each in a different muted color (charcoal, sand, slate blue, sage), each with one plain text label in black: "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek". Thin lines run from all four tiles into one larger white tile on the right that holds the attached MODUS logo. Soft daylight from the left, gentle shadows, lots of empty space at the top. Large bold black headline at the top reading exactly: "4 models. 1 platform." Small text at the bottom reading exactly: "MODUS. $28 a month."
**Why:** the core idea in one glance. It's the control ad every other concept gets measured against.

## B2. Remote control

On the left cushion, four different TV remotes are scattered messily, each with a small white tape label handwritten in black marker: "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek". On the right cushion sits one sleek single remote with the attached MODUS logo on it. Warm evening lamp light, shallow depth of field, real-home feel. Bold black text across the top reading exactly: "You don't need four remotes."
**Why:** everyone instantly knows the four-remotes pain. The metaphor does the explaining.

## B6. Blind taste test
**Prompt:** Photorealistic photo of four identical white coffee cups in a row on an ivory table, each with a small handwritten paper label: "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek". ## B7. Before / after phone
**Prompt:** Split image. Left half: a hand holding a phone whose home screen is a cluttered grid of four plain gray app squares labeled in small text "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek", with notification dots. Right half: the same hand holding a phone with a clean ivory home screen and one single app icon using the attached MODUS logo. A thin vertical line divides the halves. Small text "Before" top left and "After" top right. Bold black text at the bottom reading exactly: "4 apps. 1 app."
**Why:** before/after is one of the most reliable static formats on Meta.
