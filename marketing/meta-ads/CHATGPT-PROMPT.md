# Prompt for ChatGPT

## Steps
1. Open a new ChatGPT chat.
2. Attach these files from `reference/`:
   - `logo.png` (the mark)
   - `logo-with-text.png` (the mark with "MODUS PILOT", same as the site's navbar)
   - `ui-model-picker.png` (real screenshot: DeepSeek, GPT, Claude and Gemini on the $24 plan)
   - `ui-compare-3-models.png` (real screenshot: GPT, Claude and Gemini answering side by side)
3. Attach the concept file for the audience you're doing, for example `prompts/broad.md`.
4. Paste everything between the lines below.

---

You are my ad designer for MODUS, an app that puts GPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek in one platform for $24 a month.

I attached the MODUS logo files, two real screenshots of the app and a file of ad concepts. Make the images one at a time, in order, starting with the first concept in the file.

Rules for every image:
- Portrait 1024x1536 (I will crop to 4:5 for the feed).
- Use my attached MODUS logo exactly as provided. Do not redraw, recolor or restyle it.
- Colors: warm ivory background #F0EEE6, near-black text #141413, violet #7C3AED for small accents only.
- Put the on-image text EXACTLY as written in quotes in the concept. Spell every word correctly. Big, bold and readable on a phone.
- No other company's logo or app icon. Model names (GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek) appear as plain text only.
- Never invent a fake MODUS app screen. If a concept says [REAL UI] or shows a screen, place my attached screenshot on the screen exactly as it is, without retyping or changing its text.
- No em dashes, no emoji.
- Photos should look real and candid, not like stock photos.

After each image, tell me the concept ID (like B1) and wait for me to say "next" or give changes.

---

## If something comes out wrong
- Misspelled text: "Fix the spelling of the text, keep everything else the same."
- Logo got redrawn: "Use my attached logo file exactly, do not redraw it."
- Looks like stock photography: "Make it look like a real candid phone photo, natural light, slight grain."
- Want another version: "Same concept, new background color and a different headline from copy.md."
