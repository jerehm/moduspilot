# Students (18 to 24, college)

Paste the Brand block from `README.md` under every prompt. Copy comes from `copy.md` > Students.

## S1. Finals week, 2am
**Prompt:** Candid photorealistic photo of a dorm desk at 2am lit by a warm desk lamp and laptop glow. Energy drink can, highlighters, open textbook, sticky notes. The laptop screen is softly out of focus. Slight film grain, authentic not staged. Bold white text across the dark upper area reading exactly: "Finals week. 4 AIs. 1 tab." Small white text at the bottom reading exactly: "GPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek in MODUS."
**Why:** a moment students recognize instantly, with the angle in six words.

## S2. Study group
**Prompt:** Photorealistic photo of four college students around a library table, each on their own laptop. Above each head floats a small speech bubble with plain text: "GPT says...", "Claude says...", "Gemini says...", "DeepSeek says...". Natural library light, friendly and real, diverse group. Bold black text on a clean ivory band at the top reading exactly: "Your study group, in one app."
**Why:** turns the comparison idea into a social scene.

## S3. Three answers, one question [REAL UI optional]
**Prompt:** Overhead flat lay on an ivory desk: three lined notebook pages side by side, each handwritten in a different pen color with a short different answer to the same math problem. A small sticky note on each page reads "GPT", "Claude" and "Gemini". A yellow highlighter rests across the best one. Natural light. Bold black text at the top reading exactly: "Ask once. Compare three."
**Alt:** swap the notebooks for your real Compare screenshot placed on a laptop on the same desk.
**Why:** shows the action (comparing) instead of describing it.

## S4. Ramen budget
**Prompt:** Photorealistic photo of a student's small kitchen counter: a cup of instant ramen, a phone and a crumpled receipt listing three lines in plain type, "AI app $20", "AI app $20", "AI app $20", crossed out in red pen. Beside it a neat card reading "MODUS $24/mo" with the attached logo. Warm light, real and a little messy. Bold black text at the top reading exactly: "Why pay for three?"
**Why:** students feel subscription costs. VERIFY competitor prices first (see README).

## S5. Group chat
**Prompt:** Clean phone mockup on an ivory background showing a group chat screen in light mode. Group name "AI study group". Four short replies from contacts named "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini" and "DeepSeek", each with a plain gray circle avatar and no logos, answering the question "explain this in one line?" with four different short lines. The last message is a violet bubble from "me" reading "ok MODUS has all of you". Bold black headline above the phone reading exactly: "The group chat for AI."
**Why:** native to how students talk, and it reads like a screenshot from a friend.

## S6. Library card
**Prompt:** Close-up photo of a vintage library checkout card on a wooden desk. The borrowed column lists, in different handwriting: "GPT", "Claude", "Gemini", "DeepSeek". A violet rubber stamp across the card shows the attached MODUS logo with the word "ALL ACCESS". Warm nostalgic light. Bold black text on a clean band at the top reading exactly: "One card. Every model."
**Why:** clever and a little nostalgic, which helps it stand out.
